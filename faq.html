FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions Before You Begin?

Here are answers to some of the questions professionals commonly ask about our services, process, and approach.

GENERAL QUESTIONS

How We Work

A clear understanding of the process helps you know what to expect before starting a service.

What does Unstoppable Career Services do?

Unstoppable Career Services provides professional career strategy, positioning, career-document, executive development, and talent services. Our work helps professionals communicate their experience, achievements, leadership, and professional value more effectively.

Who are your services designed for?

Our services can support executives, senior leaders, experienced professionals, career transitioners, and other professionals who want to strengthen their career positioning and professional presentation.

Do you only work with executives?

No. While executive-level career strategy is an important part of our work, we also support experienced professionals and individuals navigating career transitions or seeking stronger professional positioning.

How does the process begin?

The process begins by understanding your professional background, career direction, objectives, and the type of support you are seeking. From there, the appropriate service and scope can be discussed.

Will my résumé be completely rewritten?

The approach depends on the service selected and the condition of your existing materials. The focus is not simply on changing words. We look at how your experience, achievements, responsibilities, and professional value are communicated.

What is Candidate Evidence Strategy (CES)?

Candidate Evidence Strategy is a structured approach to identifying, organizing, and presenting relevant professional evidence that supports a candidate's positioning for a specific career opportunity.

The scope of CES can vary depending on the candidate, opportunity, professional background, and information available.

Can you guarantee that I will get a job or interview?

No. Employment outcomes depend on many factors outside the control of any career service provider, including qualifications, competition, employer requirements, market conditions, timing, and individual hiring decisions.

Our role is to help strengthen how your professional experience and value are communicated. We do not guarantee employment, interviews, offers, promotions, or compensation.

Can you work with my existing résumé and LinkedIn profile?

Yes. Existing career materials can provide useful context about your professional history, responsibilities, achievements, and current positioning. The appropriate approach depends on the service selected.

Do you work with confidential career information?

Clients should only provide information they are comfortable sharing for the purpose of the requested service. Sensitive information should be handled thoughtfully, and clients should avoid sharing passwords, financial account credentials, or other unnecessary security information.

How long does a service take?

Timelines vary depending on the service, scope of work, information required, and level of customization. A delivery timeline can be confirmed when the service scope is established.

Can I ask questions before purchasing a service?

Yes. We encourage professionals to understand the service and scope before proceeding. You can contact our team to discuss what you are trying to accomplish and determine what type of support may be relevant.

Do you provide recruiting and talent services?

Yes. Recruiting and talent services are part of our broader professional service offering. The specific scope depends on the organization, position, hiring requirements, and engagement.
